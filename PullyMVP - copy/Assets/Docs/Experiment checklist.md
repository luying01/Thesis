Experiment checklist

1.  Change participant ID in ExperimentLogger
2.  Check or uncheck UseModel in CognitiveLoadController based on experiment need (Record whether Cognitive Level is )
3.  Check Begin Measuring in CognitiveLoadController, unless explicitly not wanting to make measurements after calibration
3.  Uncheck EndCalibrationEarly in PupilBaselineCalibrator
4.  Set number of desired calibration seconds in PupilBaselineCalibrator
5.  Explain the experiment setup to the participant and what they should expect
6.  Ensure headset fit on participant
7.  Make the participant do the Eye tracking calibration in headset settings (Give instructions on where)
8.  Boot up the game and ensure working conditions
9.  Have the participant relax for the calibration period (use EndCalibrationEarly if necessary)
 -  After the calibration period, explain controls and ensure participant understanding
 -  When Participant is ready, check Begin Measuring in CognitiveLoadController if it was unchecked
10. Make the participant perform the experiment as per the instructions until end
11. Ensure the measurements have been recorded in the folder (C:\Users\xxxx\AppData\LocalLow\DefaultCompany\PullyMVP\CognitiveLoadData)
12. Post-experiment questionaire(?)
13. Thank the participant for their time
